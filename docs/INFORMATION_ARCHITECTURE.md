# Information architecture v0.1

This map is intentionally versioned separately from the frozen Product Bible.

## User surfaces

| Surface | Primary question | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| Today | What matters today? | One active experiment and one continuation point |
| Help now | What can I safely do in this situation? | Safety route or one tolerable registered skill |
| Practice | What am I learning before the next event? | Guided rehearsal plus generalization plan |
| My protocol | What works for me, when, and with what confidence? | Evidence, uncertainty, and next experiment |
| Psychologist access | What may a professional see or change? | Granular consent, audit trail, and revocation |

## Psychologist surfaces

| Surface | Time budget | Required output |
|---|---:|---|
| Client list | 15 seconds | Risk/attention flags and meaningful change only |
| Client brief | 90 seconds | Goals, attempts, outcomes, missing links, hypotheses |
| Route editor | 2 minutes | Assign, block, prioritize, or request an experiment |
| Consent/audit | As needed | Who accessed or changed what and why |

## First clickable flow

1. User chooses Help now or Practice.
2. User describes one concrete situation and intended outcome.
3. Safety gate decides whether SKILLER may continue.
4. Formulation captures function, intensity, capacity, and change point.
5. Protocol engine offers one skill and explains why it is eligible.
6. User performs or rehearses the smallest complete version.
7. System checks completion, goal progress, immediate effect, and cost.
8. A delayed check measures durability and avoidance.
9. My protocol shows the updated evidence and next experiment.
